# Phase 3.15 — Surface Rendering & Exposure

**Status:** DRAFT — expand to READY before implementing
**Milestone:** M1 Growth loop
**Requires:** 3.14
**Unlocks:** 3.16

## Goal

An APPROVED action renders on the cart and checkout surfaces, re-validated at
render time, and the customer's view/click/dismiss is recorded as `OFFER_*`
events — the exposure that attribution depends on.

## Scope

- `lib/growth/render/get-actions-for-surface.ts` — query
  `(storeId, surface, status = APPROVED)`, filter to this customer/session,
  exclude expired
- **Execution-time re-validation** before anything reaches the page: product
  still ACTIVE and purchasable, stock still sufficient, action not expired,
  offer still within current policy versions. Writes a second
  `GuardrailEvaluation` with `phase = EXECUTION`
- `lib/growth/render/action-projection.ts` — the customer-facing shape. Like
  `StorefrontProduct` in 3.6, the absence of internal fields is **structural**
- On successful render: status → `EXECUTED`, `executedAt` set,
  `AuditEntry(AI_ACTION_EXECUTED)`
- `components/storefront/suggestion-card.tsx` — renders on cart and checkout
- `OFFER_VIEWED` on actual visibility, `OFFER_CLICKED` on interaction,
  `OFFER_DISMISSED` on explicit dismissal — each referencing `aiActionId`
- Clicking adds the target product to the cart and records
  `PRODUCT_CLICK` with `source: "AI_ACTION"`

### Exposure must mean exposure

`OFFER_VIEWED` is the precondition for revenue attribution. Firing it when the
component mounts off-screen would inflate attribution with impressions nobody
saw. Use an intersection observer, or fire on a deliberate render the customer
can actually see, and **write down which you chose and why**.

## Decisions to make at expansion

1. **Visibility definition.** As above — the honesty of every attribution number
   downstream rests on this choice.
2. **Re-validation failure.** Action expires, is cancelled, or is silently not
   rendered? `EXPIRED` and `CANCELLED` both exist in the lifecycle; pick per
   failure reason and record the mapping.
3. **Multiple approved actions on one surface.** Render the most recent, or all?
   One is simpler and avoids interfering with last-touch attribution.
4. **Dedupe of `OFFER_VIEWED`.** A cart page reloaded five times should probably
   not be five exposures. Decide, and use `clientEventId` if dedupe is wanted.

## Out of scope

`HOME`, `PRODUCT_DETAIL` and `NOTIFICATION` surfaces — M2 (3.20, 3.21).
Attribution computation — 3.16. Offer redemption at checkout — note that
`OrderItem.appliedActionId` and `appliedOfferId` exist for it (ADR-2.7-023 facts
3 and 4) and decide in 3.16 whether M1 uses them.

## Acceptance (sketch)

- With an APPROVED `CROSS_SELL` on `CART`, the cart page shows the suggestion
- Card shows live price from `Product` — change the price in the DB, reload, it updates
- **Card contains no rationale, confidence, cost or margin** — inspect the wire payload
- Setting the target product out of stock makes it disappear on reload, and a
  second `GuardrailEvaluation` with `phase = EXECUTION` records why
- Viewing produces exactly one `OFFER_VIEWED` referencing the action
- Clicking produces `OFFER_CLICKED` and adds the product to the cart
- Dismissing produces `OFFER_DISMISSED` and the card does not return
- A rejected action never renders
